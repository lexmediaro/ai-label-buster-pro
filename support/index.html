Common questions

What does the app remove?

You choose: only the AI label, the AI label plus private details (location, camera and editing apps), or all hidden details. The colors and orientation of your photo always stay the same.

Does it change my photo or video?

No. The app saves a new copy and checks that the picture, the video and the sound are exactly the same as before. Your original is never changed.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose AI Label Buster Pro. You can cancel any time; it stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought Pro on another iPhone or iPad

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account.
