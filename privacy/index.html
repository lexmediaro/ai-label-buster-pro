Privacy Policy

AI Label Buster Pro does not collect any data. Your photos and videos stay on your device.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

The short version

  • Your photos and videos are read and cleaned on your iPhone, iPad or Android device. They are never uploaded.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google Play. We only learn whether your subscription is active.
  • We don't collect, sell or share any personal data.

Who we are

AI Label Buster Pro is made by SC LEX GLOBAL IT SRL (CUI 39743639, Reg. Com. J2018000908068), Romania. Contact: lex.media@gmail.com.

Your photos and videos

The app opens only the photos and videos you choose, in the app or through the Share menu. It reads and cleans them entirely on your device. The files, their contents and their hidden details are never sent to us or to anyone else, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

The app adds to your photo library only when you tap Save to Photos (iPhone and iPad, after asking your permission) or Save to Gallery (Android). It never reads the rest of your library.

Purchases

The Pro subscription is sold and billed by Apple through the App Store, or by Google through Google Play. They handle your payment details under Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy. The app receives only whether your subscription is active, so it can unlock Pro features. We never see your name, email address or payment details from a purchase.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party services that collect data.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and keep the conversation only as long as needed to help you. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because the app collects no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you live in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you can still contact us with any question, and you have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Romania, ANSPDCP).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
