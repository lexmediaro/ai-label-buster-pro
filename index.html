Why your photo says “AI info”

One AI touch-up. One AI label.

An AI label appears because the editing app writes an AI tag into your file. Use Generative Fill, Magic Eraser, an AI enhance or an AI video edit, and the app adds a “made with AI” tag and Content Credentials to the photo. Instagram, Facebook and TikTok read that hidden metadata and label your post, even when the photo is yours. Remove the metadata and the label has nothing to read.

  1. 01

    You edit with AI

    Even a small retouch in Photoshop, Lightroom, Snapseed, CapCut or your phone’s own photo editor can mark the whole photo or video as AI-edited.

  2. 02

    The file carries the label

    The AI tag and a Content Credentials record travel inside the file, invisible in the picture, with the name of the AI tool you used.

  3. 03

    Platforms read it

    When you upload, the platform reads that data and shows “AI info” or “AI-generated”. AI Label Buster Pro removes it from your copy first.

FAQ

AI label removal: questions, answered.

How do I remove the AI label from a photo?

Remove the hidden AI metadata before you post. In AI Label Buster Pro, pick the photo and tap Remove AI Label. The app deletes the Content Credentials and AI tags that mark the photo as AI-edited, checks the picture is unchanged, and saves a clean copy to Photos. Post that copy.

How do I remove AI info on Instagram?

Instagram shows “AI info” when it finds AI metadata inside the file you upload. To remove AI info on Instagram, remove that metadata first: clean the photo or Reel with AI Label Buster Pro, save the copy, and upload the copy. A post that is already live keeps its label, so delete it and post the clean copy. If you turn on Instagram’s own AI label while posting, it still appears.

How do I remove AI info from a photo?

“AI info” comes from two things hidden in the photo file: a Content Credentials record and a “made with AI” source tag. Removing both removes the AI info. AI Label Buster Pro finds them, removes them, and leaves the pixels untouched, so the photo looks exactly the same but no longer says it was made with AI.

How do I remove the AI tag from a photo or video?

The AI tag is a line of metadata (the IPTC “digital source type”) that editing apps write after an AI edit. To remove the AI tag, open the photo or video in AI Label Buster Pro and tap remove. It works on JPEG, HEIC and PNG photos and on MP4 and MOV videos, and the video and sound stay identical.

How do I remove AI metadata from a photo on iPhone?

The iPhone Photos app can remove location, but not AI metadata. To remove AI metadata from a photo on iPhone, use an app that edits the file itself: AI Label Buster Pro removes the AI label, and optionally GPS, camera and editing-app details, then saves a clean copy. Nothing is uploaded.

Is there an AI label remover for Android?

Yes. AI Label Buster Pro is on Google Play for Android 10 and later. It works the same as on iPhone: pick photos or videos from the gallery or Files, or share them from Google Photos, CapCut and other apps, then save a verified clean copy to your gallery. The same 3-day free trial and $4.99 per month apply.

What is C2PA, and is this a C2PA remover?

C2PA, shown to people as Content Credentials, is a standard that records how a file was made or edited, including AI edits, in a signed record inside the file (see Content Credentials). Yes, AI Label Buster Pro is a C2PA remover: it removes that record from JPEG, HEIC, PNG, MP4 and MOV files, along with IPTC AI tags.

Does removing the AI label change my photo or video?

No. Only the hidden metadata changes. The compressed image data, every video frame and the sound are copied byte for byte, and the app verifies this before you save. Colors, orientation, resolution and quality stay the same.

What can’t it remove?

AI Label Buster Pro removes the AI label stored in a file’s hidden metadata. It can’t remove invisible watermarks woven into the pixels (such as Google’s SynthID), labels a platform adds from its own detection, or a label you switch on yourself when posting. It’s made for your own photos and videos, not for passing off AI-generated images as real.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. AI Label Buster Pro works entirely on your iPhone or iPad. It has no accounts, analytics or tracking. See the privacy policy.
