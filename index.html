Why your photo says “AI info”

One AI touch-up. One AI label.

When you use Generative Fill, Magic Eraser, an AI enhance or an AI video edit, the app quietly writes an AI tag and Content Credentials into the file. Instagram, Facebook and TikTok read that hidden metadata and add a label to your post, even when the photo is yours. To remove the AI label, or remove AI tags you never asked for, you remove that metadata from the file.

1

You edit with AI

Even a small retouch in Photoshop, Lightroom, Snapseed or CapCut can mark the whole photo or video as AI-edited.

2

The file carries the label

The AI tag (IPTC “digital source type”) and a Content Credentials manifest (C2PA) travel inside the file, invisible in the picture.

3

Platforms read it

When you upload, the platform reads that data and shows “AI info” or “AI-generated”. AI Label Buster Pro removes it from your copy first.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I remove the AI label from a photo?

Open AI Label Buster Pro, pick the photo, and tap Remove AI Label. The app removes the Content Credentials and AI tags that mark the photo as AI-edited, checks that the picture is unchanged, and saves a clean copy to Photos. Post the clean copy instead of the original.

How do I remove “AI info” on Instagram?

To remove AI info on Instagram, start before you post. Instagram reads the hidden AI metadata inside your photo or video when you upload it. Remove the AI label with AI Label Buster Pro first, save the clean copy, and upload that copy. A post that is already published keeps its label, so delete it and post the clean copy.

Does removing the AI label change my photo or video?

No. Only the hidden metadata changes. The compressed image data, every video frame and the sound are copied byte for byte, and the app verifies this before you save. Colors, orientation, resolution and quality stay the same.

What is C2PA, and is this a C2PA remover?

C2PA (Content Credentials) is a standard that records how a file was made or edited, including AI edits, in a signed manifest inside the file. Yes: AI Label Buster Pro removes the C2PA manifest from JPEG, HEIC, PNG, MP4 and MOV files, along with IPTC AI source tags.

Can I remove the AI tag from videos too?

Yes, you can remove the AI tag from videos. MP4 and MOV videos from your iPhone, CapCut and other editors work the same way: the AI tag, Content Credentials, location and camera details are removed, and the video and sound stay identical.

What other metadata can it remove?

Besides the AI label, it can remove GPS location, camera and phone model, editing apps and history, dates, names, copyright and captions. You choose with three modes: AI label only, AI label plus private info, or everything.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. AI Label Buster Pro works entirely on your iPhone. It has no accounts, analytics or tracking. See the privacy policy.
